Where to look, if you are stuck

  • Brand: the About page, founder interviews, packaging copy, the price list.
  • Genre: the last twenty posts on the main account, plus the website gallery.
  • Concept: campaign pages, seasonal collections, and the captions that introduce them.
  • Strategy: press releases, news coverage, collaboration announcements, store openings.
  • Platform: one product followed across the feed, short video, and the product page.
  • Brief: the images themselves: repeated crops, recurring props, consistent light, and what never appears.
  • Story: campaign films, the sequence of the feed, the order of images down a landing page.
