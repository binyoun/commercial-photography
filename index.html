Every week

Session rhythm

The same three-hour rhythm carries every topic, and the closing upload builds your learning journal week by week.

  1. 00:00ReviewLast session's uploads on screen. What worked, what did not, and why.
  2. 00:15LectureOne idea, built on live brand cases.
  3. 01:00DemoThe setup of the day, built once in front of everyone.
  4. 01:15Studio90 minutes of shooting. Teams build the setup; the instructor rotates.
  5. 02:45UploadOne selected frame with one line on intent. It becomes a page of your journal (A1).
How you are assessed

Assessments

Three assessments, none of them written in a panic at the end: the weekly rhythm produces them. Submit on Canvas; due dates are set there each semester.

A1 · Built weekly 40%

Visual Learning Journal

The upload ritual at 02:45 is the journal. Each entry: the selected frame, the setup, and one line on intent. Checkpoint in session 05.

A2 · Sessions 03 to 12 40%

Final Project from Brief

Seeded in session 03, developed in 07 and 08 (treatment, brand kit, moodboard), shot from 09, reviewed in 10 and 11, pitched client-style in 12.

A3 · With A2 20%

Critical Reflection

Reflects on your A2 project: decisions, references, AI use declared, and what changed between treatment and delivery. Your weekly journal lines are its raw material.

Rules and tips

  • Upload every weekOne frame and one line on intent at the end of each session. Skip it and A1 has a hole.
  • Declare any AI useFollow the AI ladder in S03: craft first. Any AI in your process is declared in A3.
  • Build it in our studioEvery moodboard frame must be something you can actually light and shoot in the RMIT studio.
  • Canvas winsDates, rooms and submission details are on Canvas. If anything here differs, Canvas is correct.
